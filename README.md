# Landen Makowski | Portfolio

## About This Project

This repository contains my personal portfolio website which highlights my background in Criminal Justice and Information Science, with a focus on open-source intelligence (OSINT), cybersecurity, and intelligence analysis.

I'm excited to showcase walkthroughs of different OSINT investigations and projects that I have worked on.

## Featured Project: OSINT CTF Walkthrough

The featured walkthrough will document a completed OSINT challenge, including:

- The initial scenario and investigative objective.
- The publicly available information provided.
- Tools and research techniques used during the investigation.
- Key findings and supporting evidence.
- The process of verifying information and reaching a conclusion.
- Lessons learned throughout the investigation.

The goal is to demonstrate the reasoning, research process, and methods used to solve the challenge, not just the answer.

## Future Development

As the portfolio develops, I plan to expand it with additional intelligence-related projects, academic work, technical skills, and professional experience.

The long-term goal is to create a professional portfolio that demonstrates both my web development abilities and my interests in intelligence and security.

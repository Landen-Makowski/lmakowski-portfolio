    What is geolocation?

    Geolocation involves identifying the geographic location associated with an image, video, or other piece of information. In OSINT investigations, this can involve examining landmarks, street signs, architecture, maps, and satellite imagery to determine where something occurred.

  • Image Analysis
    What is image analysis?

    Image analysis involves examining visual information to identify useful details and investigative leads. This can include analyzing objects, backgrounds, metadata, and other visual clues to verify information or support an investigation.
